---
title: Requirements
description: What you need before installing.
---

- REAPER 7.x. Earlier versions may work, but are not tested.
- [ReaImGui](https://github.com/cfillion/reaimgui) 0.10 or newer.

Nothing else. Version 2.1 and earlier needed Lokasenna's GUI library v2 and
SWS. Version 3.0 needs neither.
